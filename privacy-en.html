Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Pole Memory ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile application that helps pole dancers record, organize, and browse their training videos. We take the protection of your personal data very seriously.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following data:

  • Email address — used for account creation and authentication.
  • Videos — video files you choose to sync from your device gallery to your Pole Memory library.
  • Thumbnails — automatically generated from your videos for display in the app.
  • Video metadata — title, description, tags, categories, duration, recording date, file size.
  • Usage data — storage space used, plan type (free/pro).

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any location data.
  • We do not collect any contact information.
  • We do not use any advertising trackers (no Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and secure your account.
  • Store and display your videos in your personal library.
  • Allow you to organize your videos with tags and categories.
  • Calculate and display your storage usage.

4. Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on Supabase (infrastructure hosted in Europe).
  • Videos are stored in a private bucket — only you can access them via temporary signed URLs.
  • All communications are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored on your device (Secure Store).

5. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties. Your videos are strictly private and accessible only from your account.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your videos at any time from within the app. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access — view the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct your personal data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and data.
  • Portability — obtain a copy of your data in a structured format.

8. App Permissions

  • Gallery / Photos — to access your local videos and sync them.
  • Internet — to communicate with our servers (authentication, upload, sync).

9. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If significant changes are made, we will notify you through the app.
